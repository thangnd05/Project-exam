## Bảng dấu hiệu thì

| Dấu hiệu | Thì |
|----------|-----|
| yesterday, last week, ago | **Past simple** |
| since, for, already, yet | **Present perfect** |
| by the end of last month/year | **Past perfect** |
| tomorrow, next week | **Future** (will / be going to) |
| while, when + past | **Past continuous** |
| every day, usually | **Present simple** |
| now, currently | **Present continuous** |

## Chủ vị ngữ — Bẫy kinh điển

| Câu | Động từ |
|-----|---------|
| **The number of** applicants ___ increasing. | **is** (chủ ngữ = The number) |
| **A number of** employees ___ absent. | **are** (chủ ngữ = employees) |

## Thì trong ngữ cảnh công sở

| Ngữ cảnh | Thì | Ví dụ |
|----------|-----|-------|
| Thói quen | Present simple | The office **opens** at 9. |
| Đang diễn ra | Present continuous | We **are reviewing** applications. |
| Kinh nghiệm | Present perfect | She **has worked** here for 5 years. |
| Lịch sử | Past simple | The company **was founded** in 1990. |
| Trước quá khứ | Past perfect | He **had left** before the meeting started. |
| Tương lai | will / be going to | The shipment **will arrive** tomorrow. |

## Part 6

- Đọc câu trước/sau để xác định thì trong **ngữ cảnh đoạn văn**.
- Email thông báo sự kiện đã xảy ra → past; kế hoạch tương lai → future.

## Mini-practice

The marketing team ___ the campaign results by the end of last month.

- (A) analyzes
- (B) has analyzed
- (C) had analyzed
- (D) will analyze

**Đáp án: (C)** — by the end of **last month** → Past perfect.

---

A number of technical issues ___ reported during the software update.

- (A) was
- (B) were
- (C) has been
- (D) is

**Đáp án: (B)** — A number of + N số nhiều → were.
