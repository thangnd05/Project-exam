## Liên từ

| Liên từ | Cấu trúc | Nghĩa |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Although / Even though** | + clause | Mặc dù |
| **Because / Since** | + clause | Vì |
| **Despite / In spite of** | + N / V-ing | Mặc dù |
| **If** | + clause | Nếu |
| **Unless** | + clause | Trừ khi |
| **Until** | + clause | Cho đến khi |

## Despite vs Although — Bẫy kinh điển

> ___ the rain, the event continued.  
> Bẫy: Although (cần clause: Although it rained)  
> Đúng: **Despite** (Despite the rain)

## Giới từ cố định (collocation)

| Cụm | Giới từ |
|-----|---------|
| comply | **with** |
| responsible | **for** |
| interested | **in** |
| attend | **to** |
| by the end of | **by** (deadline) |

## Part 6 — Khác biệt

- Chú ý **mối quan hệ giữa các câu** trong đoạn.
- Câu trước positive → câu sau negative → cần **However / Although**.

## Mini-practice

All employees must comply ___ the new safety regulations.

- (A) to
- (B) with
- (C) for
- (D) by

**Đáp án: (B)** — comply **with**.

---

___ the heavy rain, the outdoor concert was not canceled.

- (A) Although
- (B) Despite
- (C) Because
- (D) However

**Đáp án: (B)** — Despite + N.
