# Tag: Phủ định (Negative)

## Chiến thuật

Câu có **not, never, hardly**. *Isn't...?* → **No** = xác nhận phủ định.

## Mini-practice

**Nghe:** "Haven't you submitted the expense report yet?"

- (C) No, I'll finish it by the end of the day. ✅
- (A) Yes, I haven't turned it in yet. ❌ (mâu thuẫn ngữ pháp)
